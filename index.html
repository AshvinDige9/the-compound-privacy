The Compound – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game The Compound ("the game", "we") handles, and why. The game is a single-player tycoon / city-builder for Android.

Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your progress (cash, buildings, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive, sell or share it.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for in-game time tickets. Ads are served through the Yodo1 MAS SDK, which works with ad partners such as Google AdMob and AppLovin. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system, language, approximate location from your IP address),
  • how you interact with ads.

When the game first starts, a consent dialog from the ad SDK lets you choose whether ads may be personalised, where the law requires it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings. This data is handled under the ad partners' own policies, starting with Yodo1's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device when your time tickets have refilled. They are created on the device; no data is sent anywhere for them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

In-app purchases

This version has no real-money purchases.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security, retention and deletion

Your save file stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by ad partners is kept and deleted under their policies; you can limit it with the advertising ID settings above.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data practices change, and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/AshvinDige9/the-compound-privacy/issues, or use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
